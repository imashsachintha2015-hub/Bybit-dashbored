Drawing Tools Guide

Lines & Rays

Click the 2nd icon on the left toolbar to draw Trendlines, Horizontal Support/Resistance Rays, and Arrows.

Squares & Rectangles (Value Area / Ranges)

Click the 4th icon (brush/shapes) to select Rectangle. Ideal for drawing CME Value Areas (VAH/VAL) and consolidation boxes.

Fibonacci & Pitchforks

Click the 3rd icon to place Fibonacci Retracement, Trend-Based Extension, and Gann boxes.

Mobile Touch Gestures

Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom. Drag with 1 finger to pan across time. Tap any drawn line or square to reveal drag-handles or delete.

Indicators & Studies

Tap to toggle indicators on the chart. Changes reload instantly with your preferences preserved.
