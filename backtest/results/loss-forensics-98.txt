ADDENDUM — the Part IX "contradiction" is mostly a universe change, not a
result that failed to reproduce.

The -0.179R headline pools twelve symbols. Split by whether a symbol was in
Part IX's universe at all:

  Part IX core 4 (BTC/ETH/SOL/DOGE)   n=19   totalR  +1.0   expR +0.051
  8 symbols added for this run        n=79   totalR -18.5   expR -0.234

Part IX's own four symbols stay POSITIVE on this window. The loss comes from
symbols Part IX never traded, which were added here only to reach a ~100-trade
sample. Reporting "the deployed configuration loses money" without that split
overstated the case: the configuration was not retested, the universe was.

What this does NOT establish is that Part IX reproduces -- n=19 is far too
small to confirm +0.155R, and +0.051R is well below it.

Nor is it a clean liquidity story, despite Part XVIII's gradient. Within the
added eight, the two BEST performers are BCH (+9.5R, n=5) and ATOM (+7.4R,
n=19), both low liquidity; excluding just those two drops the remaining six to
-0.644R expectancy. An aggregate that swings that far on two symbols of five
to nineteen trades is not evidence about liquidity or about anything else.

Unaffected by all of the above: the stop-geometry finding. Median bar range of
1.39R while in trade is measured across 82 trades and is a property of the
stop distance versus volatility, not a win/loss statistic, so it does not move
with universe choice.
