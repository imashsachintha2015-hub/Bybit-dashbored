Every minute, for each volume-confirmed strong-break setup the server places the same order as "Place order" (post-only limit entry, limit take-profit, stop-market stop) within your limits, keeps each waiting order equal to its setup (the impulse extended: moved to the new levels; the setup ended: cancelled, the way the backtest treats it) and, if ticked, closes an open trade at the market (like the stop) or cancels a waiting order once BTC has moved 0.5R against it. The risk limit counts only OPEN trades: waiting orders do not use it, because most of them never fill (about 7 in 10 radar setups end unfilled); how many may wait has its own limit, and when fills use the risk limit up, the waiting orders that could not be taken are cancelled and placed again once there is room. It does not cancel an order just because the price is falling or a trend flipped: on 4,258 backtested trades that gained nothing, because the fall is what fills a pullback order. One order per coin; coins where you hold a position or your own order are skipped. Orders are calculated on the account size above (20 USDT), not on the account's balance: Bybit's smallest order is about 5 USDT, so at 20 USDT a trade needs about 0.5-1% risk and a few coins (BTC, ETH, SOL, SUI) are skipped as too small. Preview shows every decision without sending anything. Demo or testnet accounts only, no password. Every trade has its stop and target on Bybit, so it is protected even if this server stops.
